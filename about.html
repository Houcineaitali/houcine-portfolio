About Hi, I'm Houcine Ait Ali

Innovating at the intersection of AI and Web Development.

I am a Computer Science student at the Polydisciplinary Faculty of Taroudant (FPT) with a passion for turning complex problems into automated solutions. From competing in international developer competitions to building intelligent financial tools, I thrive on the challenge of integrating the latest AI capabilities into functional web applications.

How I Got Here

My journey began with a fascination for how code can bridge the gap between human imagination and reality. Starting with a 300-hour Responsive Web Design certification from freeCodeCamp, I built a solid foundation in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. However, the rise of Large Language Models (LLMs) changed everything for me. I realized that the future isn't just about building sites—it's about building intelligence. This led me to explore n8n automation, prompt engineering, and AI-driven media, eventually leading to my recognition in the Huawei Developer Competition 2025.

My Values & Beliefs

INNOVATION

I don't just use technology; I experiment with it. Whether it's RunwayML for video or GPT for market analysis, I believe in staying at the bleeding edge of what's possible.

AUTOMATION

If a task can be automated, it should be. I focus on building n8n workflows and API integrations that save time and reduce human error, as seen in my project Eventsal.

PRECISION

Coming from a background in C and C++, I value clean, efficient code. I believe that high-level AI features are only as good as the low-level logic supporting them.

CONTINUOUS LEARNING

The tech world moves fast. With certifications from IBM and the University of North Florida, I am committed to constant growth and adapting to new industrial standards.
